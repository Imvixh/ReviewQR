Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about how ReviewQR works and keeps your business Google-compliant.

What is ReviewQR? expand_more
ReviewQR is an in-store review enablement platform. We provide beautiful, print-ready tabletop stands, counter cards, and smart QR endpoints that guide happy physical customers directly to your Google Business Profile to leave genuine reviews.
Do customers need to download an app or log in? Toggle expand_more
No. Guests simply open their smartphone camera app, scan your stand's QR code, and are instantly taken to the mobile-friendly web review assistant in Safari, Chrome, or their default browser. No account registration or download is ever required.
Can customers freely edit their review before posting? expand_more
Yes, 100%. ReviewQR assists the customer by offering optional suggestions to cure writer's block. Customers always post directly through Google's native review form and retain full control to edit their stars, photos, and commentary.
Does ReviewQR automatically post reviews on Google? expand_more
No software can legally submit Google reviews on a customer's behalf. Google requires that the authenticated user submits their own review from their personal Google Account. ReviewQR makes this transition frictionless with automatic link opening and one-tap clipboard copy.
What formats do the printable stands export to? expand_more
You can export crisp 300-DPI print-ready PDFs with crop marks for standard acrylic dimensions (4×6 in, 5×7 in, A5, and A6), as well as transparent PNGs and SVGs for your own custom signage or marketing collateral.
